#### Backrooms
[Backrooms: Is it possible to escape the capitalist labyrinth?](https://evanxmerz.com/post/is-it-possible-to-escape-the-capitalist-labyrinth/)

A software engineer's review of the movie [The Backrooms](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt33508453/).

> I also related to all the ways that the characters tried to escape the labyrinth. Every way out was an illusion. Each new door only led to more rooms with more uncanny representations of office building despair. This reminded me of all the ways that we try to escape our lives within capitalism by making enough money that we no longer have to work. One of my friends tells me that she's only working her high paying corporate job as a means to an end. Another friend of mine bought houses in Buffalo and became a landlord. I chose to buy this cabin in the woods, and spend my weekends cosplaying as a survivalist.

> A horror movie like Backrooms can be weirdly comforting. Not because it isn't scary; I was certainly holding my breath at several points in the movie. But it's comforting because it's so relatable. It reminds me that I'm not going through all of this alone. Whether the director intended the bleak capitalist symbolism or not, it made me feel seen.
