#### GenZ protests
[Gen Z are taking to the streets around the world – here’s why](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/71193/1/gen-z-protests-paris-france-albania-belgium-italy-chile-riots)

> Something’s happening in the streets. This weekend, the world’s youth seemingly erupted into protest: teenagers and students mobilised across France and Belgium; young Albanians called for their prime minister’s resignation; and India’s youth-led Cockroach movement continued to challenge controversial changes to voter rolls. In Santiago, protesters marked the anniversary of Chile’s 2019 uprising by marching against government spending cuts. Elsewhere, crowds packed the streets of Lisbon and Milan to protest soaring living costs, while demonstrations in Brazil and the US turned attention towards democracy.

> From classrooms to the ballot box, we explore what’s driving the unrest – and what protesters are demanding.
