#### Louise Carmen
[The Birkin of Leather Journals](https://www.thecut.com/article/louise-carmen-leather-journals.html) #notebooks

Learned of a new brand today. Before this, the only real brand in my headspace around journals was [TRAVELER’S COMPANY - トラベラーズカンパニー](https://www.travelers-company.com/)

> For the journaling community, a [Louise Carmen](https://louisecarmen.com/) has become a coveted accessory. But for those unfamiliar, a leather journal that runs the same price as a midrange designer bag might come as something of a shock.
> 
> Fans can customize the notebooks, starting at $160 for a basic version of the trifold leather cover, with embossing, different styles of paper, and charms, which can run hundreds of dollars more. Multiple thin notebooks fit neatly inside the reusable leather cover, secured in place by elastic bands, and can be swapped out and rearranged indefinitely. Those who buy it see the journal folio as an heirloom worth investing in.
> 
> The French artisanal-leather stationery and notebook brand was founded in 2016 by Nathalie Valmary; her husband, Fabien; and their daughter, Victoire and has steadily grown in popularity via social media. [Videos](https://www.tiktok.com/@girlandgallery/video/7567098576124382486) documenting the purchasing experience often gain hundreds of thousands of views, and Americans are now responsible for 60 percent of the brand’s online orders, Valmary told [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/make-it/2026/04/09/louise-carmen-notebooks-young-americans-journaling-trend.html) earlier this year.
