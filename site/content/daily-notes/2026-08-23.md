#### Hyrox and Germany
[How Germany's Hyrox became a global fitness craze](https://www.dw.com/en/hyrox-germany-sport-running-fitness-health-strength-training/a-78074218?maca=en-newsletter_en_gns-16215-xml-newsletter&at_medium=Newsletter&at_campaign=EN%20-%20German%20News%20Service&at_dw_language=en&at_number=20260821&r=57473012265900748&lid=4012648&pm_ln=351855)

til Hyrox was created in Germany

> The format was developed by event organizer Christian Toetzke, communications expert Michael Trautmann, and Moritz Fürste, a former member of the German national field hockey team that won gold at the 2008 and 2012 Summer Olympics.
> 
> Their basic idea was very simple: to create a competition that combines the atmosphere of a running event with the intensity of functional strength training that many [people know from time in the gym](https://www.dw.com/en/the-german-gym-fighting-the-far-right-with-mma/a-77291434). Hyrox consists of eight functional stations interspersed by eight 1-kilometer (0.62 miles) runs.
