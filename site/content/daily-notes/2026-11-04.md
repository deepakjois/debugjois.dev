#### Finding my thing
[if you feel behind right now don’t worry you aren’t](https://substack.com/inbox/post/223264812?utm_source=email&redirect=app-store-no-desktop&inbox=true&utm_campaign=email-read-in-app&triedRedirect=true)

this resonated with me today morning

> But really I’m trying to find something. My _thing_. And if I do what I’ve done, I won’t find it. I need to change it up. So from now until the end of the year, the formats will vary, the content, even the days + frequency of when I post. I’m in experimental mode. The digital equivalent of going to Thailand, slinging on those awful trousers and finding myself.
> 
> I need to post what I’m interested in. For me to continue doing this for a long time, I have to make these for me. So what am I interested in?


> I’ve stopped using Grok Bot for everything, except to ping me when Carlos Alcaraz is playing. I’m reading more long-form. I’m consuming less slop (and spending a little less time on X). I’m recording a voice-note every morning for a minimum of 10 minutes, and not doing anything with the transcript. I’m letting things percolate, noticing what I come back to.
> 
> Agents give you answers to everything. But if we’re ‘soul-searching’ or trying to connect the dots, an answer presented to us may not be the right path. Even if it’s a reasonable one. All I’m ‘optimising’ for here is - what do I really want to do? And noticing what I keep coming back to when I take the time to think on it.
> 
> This is not an ‘anti-ai’ stance at all. I love AI. I can do so much more than I ever could. It’s a tool though, not a person, and I use it as such. You probably need a grand total of one tool (an agent). Instead of looking at everything as an opportunity to optimise, I want to use AI for fun.

*I want to use AI for fun* . YES!
