#### Fade Farms
[The Farms Where Vintage T-Shirts Grow](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/04/style/fade-farms-vintage-tees-clothing.html) #clothing 

> The warmer climate in England this past summer gave rise to the cultivation of some unlikely crops: [tea, cantaloupe](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/09/world/europe/england-climate-tea-cantaloupe.html) and vintage T-shirts.
> 
> William Cotterill has been tending to this last variety on a 15-acre plot of land about a half-hour from his shop, Millennial Vintage, in Stockport, England. The field is dotted with crucifix-like posts draped in old band tees, which he hoses down with salt water and dries in the sun. He repeats this process twice a week for six weeks, then dismounts the garments once they’ve achieved a satisfactory level of discoloration.
> 
> Admiring his handiwork, he said he’s had “fantastic harvests” this year on his “fade farm.”
