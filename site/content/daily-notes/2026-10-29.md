#### Work-life balance
[There’s no such thing as “work-life balance”](https://postanly.substack.com/p/theres-no-such-thing-as-work-life)

> Work-life balance is unrealistic. That’s why I aim for satisfaction. And making practical progress in these three domains: health, work, and relationships. The time split is uneven. But that doesn’t matter. Because balance is the wrong target. The goal is to feel good in all three areas. I try to protect what matters in my health and relationships. And do my best work. The fundamental rule is full presence. I’ve stopped measuring myself against a standard I can’t sustain.
