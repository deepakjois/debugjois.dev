#### Letting Go
[What it actually means to let go](https://sashachapin.substack.com/p/what-it-actually-means-to-let-go)
> For the sake of this discussion, let’s divide the self into two parts: the elephant and the rider. The elephant is our emotional/instinctive/physical/unconscious self. The rider is our cognitive/rational/planning/judging self.
>
> For many people, this allowing motion is most familiar from moments of physical or social abandon. Like:
>
> - Dancing intuitively
> - Saying the stupid joke that floats into your mind as soon as it arises
> - Hitting things with a baseball bat in a rage room
> - Surrendering into intense physical intimacy
> - Allowing your body to “walk itself” during a long walk
> - Falling into a rhythm in exercise
> 
> Though different in mood and intensity, all of these examples share the feeling of yielding to the process. This is what you’re looking for. Yield to the flow of experience, while remaining still. Allow breathing, feeling, perceiving, to be as they want to be.
